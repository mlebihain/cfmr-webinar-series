[cfmr]
description:
  The Comité Français de Mécanique des Roches (CFMR) was founded in 1967 to promote research in rock mechanics, rock physics and geotechnics, and to share its results.

  The CFMR organizes regular technical meetings, bringing together researchers, engineers and industry professionals to present their work and discuss scientific and practical problems. It also supports young researchers through the Pierre Londe Prize, awarded each year for an outstanding PhD thesis. 

  The CFMR is the French national group of the International Society for Rock Mechanics (ISRM) and maintains close links with the international rock mechanics community. Researchers, engineers, students and practitioners are welcome to join the association. When applying for membership, you can list Lucille Carbillet and Mathias Lebihain as your sponsors (parrains).
