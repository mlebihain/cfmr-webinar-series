CFMR Webinar Series

The Comité Français de Mécanique des Roches (CFMR) is a French association founded in 1967 to support research and share knowledge in rock mechanics, rock physics and geotechnics.

Our webinars bring together researchers, academics, and engineers to discuss their work, share experiences and explore current questions in rock mechanics.
